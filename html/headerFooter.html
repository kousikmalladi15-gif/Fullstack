Welcome to Braincodez

Braincodez is a edullm venture by Kousik Malladi who always believed that education shall be accessible to everyone and everyone, carrying the legacy of his father, who was a Mathematics Teacher. He belives that for one to truly learn, one must understand things in his own style for which what he learns should be taught in the ways of one's understanding, for which he tried to invent a hybrid of transformers and reinforcement learning through which AI generates the learning material and upon understanding the user's methods of learning, we can iteratively reinforce the model to speak the understanding that is native to the end learner, reducing the learning gap and the high skills that the companies require.
